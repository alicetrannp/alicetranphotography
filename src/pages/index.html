Welcome to Alice Tran Photography!

I am so HAPPY you are here! :) My name is Alice and I am a Washington DC and Los Angeles wedding photographer. To me, it has been a privilege to be able to connect with everyone that I had a chance to photograph. My goal is to capture those raw, honest, and authentic emotions so that twenty years from now, you can look back on these photos and still remember exactly how that moment felt. I hope we share the same goal and that I am the photographer you are looking for. Someone who you can trust to capture the real you, to make you feel comfortable enough to fully express yourself, even that silly, quirky and awkward side of you... I really can't wait to meet you & your loved ones and create incredible artworks together!

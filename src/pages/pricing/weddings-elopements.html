A step-by-step guide on booking a Wedding with me!

  1. Step 1/6: Phone Consultation

    After your initial inquiry, the first thing we will do is to hop on the phone and talk about you, your ideas, inspirations and your photography needs. And because you deserve the VERY best, I want to make sure that we are a good fit for each other.

  2. Step 2/6: Proposal & Contract

    After our consultation, you pick the package that most resonates with you. Then we will finalize the package details and also lock in on the date & time together. Once you sign the contract & pay the retainer, it's official!!! We are in this together!!

  3. Step 3/6: Before the Big Day

    We will discuss and finalize any important details & timeline along the way. If it's included in your package, let's do an EPIC engagement session. We will create amazing photos and videos that can be used for your Save the Date invites & more.

[[img src="pricing__weddings-elopements_099" alt="Bridesmaids in blue dresses getting ready" sizes="(min-width: 960px) 40vw, 100vw"]]

  1. Step 4/6: The Wedding Day!!!!

    How exciting! On this day, I want you to enjoy it as much as you can. I will make sure we have all the important shots and details you asked for. So just breathe and allow me to make the best day of your life into tangible, lasting memories forever. I GOT YOU!

  2. Step 5/6: Gallery Reveal

    Within 8 to 12 weeks after your wedding, you will receive your entire wedding gallery to view, share, download and most important of all, RELIVE this day all over again! :)

  3. Step 6/6: What's Next?

    I mean... by now, we are basically friends right? Our relationship can't just end after I deliver your photos! I want to see you grow as a couple and hear all about your married life adventures AND if you decide to add another member to your family, I would be honored to document these milestones of your growing family. :)

Meet Alice

Hi there, I'm Alice!

Published Photographer. Nurse Practitioner. Mental Health Advocate. Dog Lover & Forever Student!

Let me start with the fact that I wholeheartedly love photography as long as I could remember. I really do pour 100% of who I am as an artist into every photo session you have with me. I want to capture LOVE in all of its forms: candid, spontaneous, adventurous, authentic, intentional, and most of all... honest. I started experimenting with photography back when I was in high school, but didn't do it professionally until I was in college working as a photographer/ videographer in the Social Media Department. In 2019, I took a big break to pursue my nursing career. Good news is: I am finally done with my degrees, so it's time to get back to this lifelong passion of mine.

Outside of this, I work full-time as a Nurse Practitioner during the weekdays & I pride myself on being an avid mental health advocate. I also love spending time with my family and friends, cooking, trying out different cuisines, hiking, and traveling... Also, did I mention that I absolutely love dogs? See photos of my dog below!!!!!!

Anyhow, I could tell you more about myself but that is not the priority here. My priority is YOU. Let me know how we can create our best memories together! Tell me more about yourself and your vision here.

I really can't wait to create incredibly beautiful artworks for you and your family for years to come!

Booking a session with me is very simple. It goes like this: you fill out a form, we chat, we meet up, we laugh, we have fun, and we create unforgettable memories together! Years from now, I want you to look at these photos and still be able to go back in time and relive those beautiful moments.

Follow me on Instagram @alicetranphotography and Facebook to see more of my recent work and also all the behind the scenes videos to get to know more about me and my clients!

Am I the Photographer for you?

1/4: CAPTURING GREAT MEMORIES

Are you passionate about capturing great moments? So am I.

I love capturing life's precious moments, no matter how big or small: your child's first step, your proposal at a place that you two met 10 years ago, the tears in your dad's eyes when he sees you in a wedding dress for the first time, and the smile of your mother when she gets to hold her first grandchild, etc. There are a lifetime of reasons to celebrate these moments AND because words are just NOT enough, I want to capture these great memories that would last a lifetime (and more) for you and your family.

Yes. Every Detail. Forever.

2/4: CANDID & HONEST STORYTELLING

I love emotion evoking images. And in order to create these images, as a photographer, I am sometimes like "a fly on the wall." I won't overly pose you or direct you to achieve a certain image but rather, I let you take in the moment, in the presence of your loved ones. I call it the "in between moments." My job is to embrace and capture such candid moments to tell your story in the most authentic and honest way possible.

And that.... is exactly how magic happens :)

3/4: TIMELESS EDITING STYLE

I would say that my style is very true to color, photojournalistic, joyful, emotional, warm and timeless.

One of my clients even told me "Your photos just OOZE JOY, Alice."

I am not a typical "light and airy" or "dark and moody" photographer. I like rich, bold and vibrant colors, adding a bit of contrast with the perfect amount of light, not too bright where it would overpower the subjects, and not too dark with tons of shadows, either.

I believe that your wedding day or your photo session DESERVES to be captured in the most HONEST way possible. So 20 years from now, you can look back at these photographs and still remember how exactly the story unfolds with all its beautiful & true to life colors.

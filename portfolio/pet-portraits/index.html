Pet Portraits

I am not sure I would call myself a pet photographer but ever since Riley came into my life, I made sure to document our journey almost every single day through my phone. Riley did grow up so fast and I always regret not taking more photos of him when he was little. Truth is ... we all hope to have our pets for a very long time, but tomorrow is never guaranteed.

So that is why I am offering this service to my clients with the hope that I can document these cherished moments with you and your pets FOREVER.
